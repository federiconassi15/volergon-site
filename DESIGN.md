# VOLERGON — Art Direction / Visual System

## Creative thesis

**We want action.** The brand is a quiet architectural space interrupted by moments of saturated energy and choreographed movement. The website is a complete company story, not a reel of one rotating geometric object.

Primary references: interactive craft, bold type and real-time sculpture of premium digital studios including Lusion. Avoid direct copies: own choreography, layout, assets and motion narrative.

## The five colors

- Canvas: `#F0F1FA` — paper-like near white, editorial space.
- Ink: `#000000` — maximum contrast and typography.
- Graphite: `#2B2E3A` — quiet supporting surfaces and labels.
- Ultramarine: `#1A2FFB` — decisive interaction and energy.
- Voltage: `#C1FF00` — rare moment of surprise, never everywhere.

The 2-neutrals + 1-energetic-color heuristic is a useful bias, not a mathematical law. The palette leaves room for white, near-black, silver and blue lighting inside rendered artwork.

## Typography

- Preferred licensed brand family: Aeonik (NOT included or required to run).
- Fallback display + body: DM Sans (Google Fonts, OFL), deliberately restrained weights, tight tracking and generous large headings.
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono, exclusively for numerical metadata and system labels.
- No all-caps monospace body copy; no fake futurist condensed display font.

## Motion

Motion must communicate hierarchy and change, not just decorate.

- Preload transition: under a second, never block content indefinitely.
- Navigation: full-screen electric-blue menu, meaningful keyboard focus.
- Hero: original fluid interlocking ribbon SDF, pearlescent light, pointer parallax, scroll-reactive pose.
- Typography: clear directional entrances and occasional large-scale spatial displacement.
- Scrolling: variable pacing, editorial stillness between intense beats.
- Interaction: tab-controlled Bureau workflow, quadrant-controlled Golden Ratio explainer.
- Reduced motion: no essential information hidden; static graphic fallback, no autoplay.

## Technical art constraints

WebGL2 is an enhancement, never a blocker. Shader is deliberately procedural and self-contained. Pixel resolution is capped. IntersectionObserver pauses rendering offscreen, visibilitychange pauses background tabs. A CSS composition remains if WebGL is missing or context is lost. No downloaded models or external render assets.

## Non-negotiable honest copy

- Volergon is a brand/project until legal company status is verified. Do not claim YC admission, investment or incorporation.
- Bureau is an AI agent platform/harness, not an LLM.
- Golden Ratio is an upcoming capability, not a shipping AI predictor.
- No fake users, paid plans, live analytics, certifications, integrations, quotes, email addresses or working APIs.
- The site is distinct from the Bureau app. Security-sensitive product behavior remains in Rust Core.

## Visual review checklist

Verify cinematic 3D actually renders; no rotating cube, no clipped headlines, no invisible links, no text obstructing product CTAs, no endless loading, no 404 routes, no mobile horizontal scroll, no inaccessible menu, no motion for users requesting reduced motion. A functional animation isn't automatically good art direction: reviewers should inspect actual screenshots and adjust composition.
